Why the ?

01

We Put Electrolytes In. No One Else Did.

Every cocktail RTD out there is basically sugar water with alcohol and vibes. We actually built our base around electrolytes — 220mg of sodium, potassium, magnesium per can. You hydrate while you drink. Wild concept. Nobody tried it. We did.

02

Desi Flavours. Zero Apology.

The flavours your NANI used. that the craft cocktail bars are now charging ₹800 for. We bottled your pantry. Nostalgia hits different when it's got 12% ABV.

03

A Cart, Not a Bar. That's the Point.

You don't need a reservation. You don't need a dress code. You just need to find the cart. Buurp is built for the street — designed to show up where the party already is, not wait for you to find it.
